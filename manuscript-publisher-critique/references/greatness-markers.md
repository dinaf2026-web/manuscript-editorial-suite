# Greatness Markers — What Makes a Great Book, by Genre

The developmental yardstick for the publisher critique. Use the **general** set
plus the overlay named by the profile's `greatness_set`. Grade each relevant
scorecard category against the marker(s) below it, and answer the compass question
at the end of every read: *which marker is this book underserving most?*

The book's `craft_standard` (from the profile) is the technique north star —
measure against that author's *technique*, never their words.

---

## GENERAL (applies to every genre)

1. **A voice readers trust.** The narration earns authority and stays consistent;
   it carries the reader through scenes the protagonist doesn't fully understand.
   *Failure:* generic or inconsistent voice; a register mismatched to the reader.
   → **Voice & POV Authority**.

2. **Characters who aren't interchangeable.** Distinct, dimensional people whose
   relationships *change* across the book. *Failure:* a static or interchangeable
   cast; a world that resets each scene. → **Character & Relationships**.

3. **Layered stakes.** At least three layers at once — the immediate problem, a
   larger arc, and a character thread *this* book tests. *Failure:* single-layer
   storytelling. → **Layered Stakes**.

4. **Architecture that pulls.** Chapters end on open beats; the cumulative effect is
   compulsion, not manipulation. *Failure:* resolved chapter ends; no forward pull.
   → **Pacing & Chapter Architecture**.

5. **A setting that does work.** Recurring sensory anchors and texture press on the
   scene; the story couldn't be relocated without loss. *Failure:* the anywhere-
   place. → **Setting / World as Character**.

6. **Emotional truth as the engine.** Readers come for the premise and return for
   the feeling; the emotional spine is threaded *inside* the action beats. If you
   can name the spine in one sentence, the book has it. *Failure:* mechanics with no
   stake beneath. → **Emotional Truth**.

7. **A causal, earned structure.** Every turn follows from character and prior
   event; nothing is solved by coincidence or convenience. *Failure:* unearned
   leaps, withheld information, out-of-character action. → **Plot & Structure Logic**.

8. **A legible promise.** From chapter one the reader knows what kind of book this is
   and trusts it to deliver. *Failure:* fuzzy positioning; a voice not ready for its
   shelf. → **Marketability & Comps**.

## OVERLAY: mystery / crime
- **Fair play above all** — clues shown, no withheld facts, no coincidence solving
  the case; surprising *and* inevitable. (Run `manuscript-fair-play-audit` for the
  deep pass.) → **Plot & Structure Logic** (renamed "Plot & Fair-Play").
- **A detective the reader earns** — a sleuth (or asymmetric pair) with a method
  the solution actually uses.
- **Proportioned danger** — peril that peaks then restores; the register's promise
  kept (cozy stays cozy; noir stays bleak honestly).
- **Plant-and-payoff discipline** — re-readable; a second read shows the seeds.
- Compass leans on: is the solution both fair and emotionally satisfying?

## OVERLAY: thriller / suspense
- **Escalating engine** — each beat raises pressure; the clock is felt.
- **Competence and jeopardy balanced** — the protagonist is capable yet genuinely at
  risk; threat is concrete, not ambient.
- **Twists that are earned, not cheated** — fairness still binds at high intensity.
- **A villain/threat with real weight.**
- → **Plot & Structure Logic** ("Plot & Tension Engine"); **Proportioned Danger**
  emphasized.

## OVERLAY: fantasy / sci-fi
- **Worldbuilding that is lived-in, not catalogued** — revealed through character
  need, not infodump. → **Setting / World as Character** (emphasis — weights stay fixed; reflect it in the grade).
- **A magic/tech system with consistent rules and costs** — the plot never bends
  them for convenience. → **Plot & Structure Logic** ("World & System Logic").
- **Wonder balanced with stakes** — awe serves the emotional arc.
- **Scope discipline** — the canon stays coherent (lean on the canon ledger).

## OVERLAY: romance
- **The relationship is the plot** — the central arc is the bond's development.
  → **Character & Relationships** (emphasis — weights stay fixed; reflect it in the grade) + **Emotional Truth** (emphasis — weights stay fixed; reflect it in the grade).
- **Earned beats** — attraction, conflict, the dark moment, and resolution each
  follow from character, not contrivance.
- **Two protagonists who change each other** — both have interiority and growth.
- **Genre promise kept** — the emotional payoff the romance reader is owed lands
  (HEA/HFN as the subgenre expects); heat level matches the shelf.
- **Tension sustained** — the keeping-apart is credible, not a misunderstanding that
  one conversation would fix.

## OVERLAY: literary / upmarket
- **Sentence-level command** — prose is part of the meaning. → **Voice** (emphasis — weights stay fixed; reflect it in the grade).
- **Interiority and theme** — the book is *about* something, carried through image
  and character, not stated. → **Emotional Truth** (emphasis — weights stay fixed; reflect it in the grade).
- **Structural intention** — form serves meaning even when non-linear.
- **Ambiguity earned, not evasive** — open questions feel deliberate, not unfinished.
- Plot logic is judged as causal plausibility and thematic coherence, not puzzle
  fairness.

## OVERLAY: memoir / narrative nonfiction
- **Narrative truth and reflection** — events carry meaning; the narrator reflects,
  not just reports. → **Emotional Truth** ("Narrative Truth & Reflection").
- **A persona the reader trusts** — honest, self-aware, not self-justifying.
  → **Voice & POV Authority**.
- **Shape imposed on life** — selection and structure turn experience into story;
  not everything that happened, only what serves the arc.
- **Stakes that are real and personal** — why this story, why now.
- **Factual integrity** — verifiable claims are accurate; the continuity pass checks
  internal consistency and timeline. The fair-play/mystery markers do **not** apply.
- **Universality through specificity** — the particular detail that opens onto the
  shared experience.

---

## The compass question
> **Which marker (general + overlay) is this book currently underserving most?**
Answer in one sentence at the end of every critique. It is the single most useful
output of the read — it tells the author where the next revision pass should go.
